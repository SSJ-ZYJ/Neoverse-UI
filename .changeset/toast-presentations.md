Add compact, action, and multiline notification presentations to Toast. All three use the same Aurora Glass surface and semantic icons. An action slot or prop selects the inline action layout by default. Persistent notifications keep their dismiss button, while timed compact and action toasts omit it.

Use the shared warning fill and contrasting glyph color for the warning icon.
