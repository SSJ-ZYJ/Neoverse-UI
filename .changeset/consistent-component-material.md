Apply Solid material to shared controls, navigation, overlays, status displays, terminal chrome, scrollbars, and Prose. Automatic surfaces follow their nearest material scope, including when an open overlay is portaled. Explicit presets keep their existing paint; explicitly setting the Tabs variant to `glass` continues to opt into Aurora Glass. The default Glass tiers and optical parameters are unchanged.
