Share material presets between the Vue and React adapters. Button, Card, and ControlSurface select their default surface from `data-neoverse-material`, with Aurora Glass as the fallback. Explicit presets remain independent of the material scope. Both optional Glass edge backends leave Solid surfaces to CSS.
